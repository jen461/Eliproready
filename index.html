Bronze
"You know the game. Now learn the profession."
Key Concepts
  • FPS (Frames Per Second) — how many images your computer generates each second. Higher FPS = smoother. 60fps is comfortable. 144fps is the competitive standard.
  • Ping — the round-trip time from your device to the game server in milliseconds. Lower is better. Under 50ms is good. Over 100ms you'll feel the delay.
  • Refresh Rate (Hz) — how many times per second your monitor updates. A 60Hz monitor only shows 60fps max, even if your computer generates 300fps. Pros use 144Hz+.
  • Match analysis isn't blame — every game tells you something. Find the turning point. Understand why it happened. That's the habit that separates players who improve from players who grind.
  • Rate your floor, not your ceiling — on the self-assessment, rate what you consistently do, not what you can sometimes pull off.

S
Silver
"Good players play. Pro players study."
Key Concepts
  • Watching vs. studying — watching for entertainment means noticing cool moments. Studying means asking WHY they made each decision. Same video, completely different result.
  • Stats are data, not judgment — 8 sessions of stat tracking shows patterns one game never can. Your notebook is a data collection tool, not a report card.
  • GPU vs CPU for gaming — the GPU renders every frame you see. The CPU handles game logic. For high FPS gaming, the GPU is typically the most important component.
  • Win rate formula — wins ÷ total games × 100. Track it weekly. Improvement only becomes visible over time.

Key Concepts
  • The World Cup numbers — 40+ million players entered qualifying for the 2019 Fortnite World Cup. 100 made the final. Bugha was 16. He won $3 million. Understanding those odds is part of understanding the path.
  • Trend vs. noise — one bad week proves nothing. Three bad weeks in a row is a pattern worth fixing. Your 4-week stat tracker is designed to reveal trends, not judge individual sessions.
